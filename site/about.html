About & data

A public record of provisional checks of statements to German federal lawmakers.

How the checks work

AI researchers read submissions, compare specific claims with primary evidence and the draft addressed by the author, and record context, competing interpretations and limitations. Each admitted finding has a separate recorded AI review. These reviews are not human specialist confirmation.

Evidence pages show the original quotations, translations, page locators, source links, correction and limits. Severity describes the materiality of a specific mismatch. Whether it favours the author’s request is a separate assessment. A correction does not by itself refute a policy request.

What the results can show

The sample is targeted and includes historical submissions examined at different depths. Complete screening of the recent three-month window has not been established. The results cannot estimate a national error rate. Reading a submission does not independently verify every assertion in it.

Unresolved leads are questions, not established errors. Rejected candidates remain in the research record. Findings are provisional; no intent is inferred. No author response or human specialist confirmation is currently recorded.

Sources can change or become unavailable. The records preserve access dates, document identities, hashes where recorded, and historical-version limits. This website build does not fetch sources or perform a new factual check.
