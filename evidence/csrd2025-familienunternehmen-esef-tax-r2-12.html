Minor error Favours the author’s request

csrd implementation 2025

Statement by Stiftung Familienunternehmen und Politik

Introducing corporate sustainability reporting and assurance rules into German commercial law.

foundation · found 25 October 2026

They want
Allow electronic formatting only at disclosure, and seek abolition of ESEF if possible.
They said
ESEF is machine-readable but not human-readable.
The source says
ESEF uses human-readable XHTML, with machine-readable tags embedded in the same report.
What it changes
Removes one technical premise supporting the request to abolish the format. Readability alone does not resolve whether preparation, tagging, verification or correction costs justify a different reporting workflow.

3. The correction

Separate technical markup from the readable report. The Commission’s 2020 explanation of the adopted regulation expressly describes a format readable by both humans and machines. ESMA explains that XHTML is viewable in ordinary web browsers and inline XBRL embeds structured tags while preserving that presentation. The July 2025 draft expressly incorporates Article 3 of Regulation 2019/815 for the report format.

4. What this does not show

No finding is made that ESEF preparation is simple, that tags can be verified by ordinary visual reading, that certified translations present no legal issues, or that a disclosure-only solution or abolition is inappropriate. The remaining legal and burden arguments are not adjudicated. No intent inferred. No claim is made that all sustainability tags were already mandatory inJuly 2025: the draft memorandum expressly envisages relevant tagging rules still to be adopted.

Minor: a definite technical description undermines a specific supporting premise, but does not remove the independent arguments about timing, tagging burden, assurance or legal workflow.
