Adding waste-prevention targets to the KrWG.

found 25 October 2026

They want
Adjust the targets to realistic conditions.
They said
The government calls a 40% reduction in municipal waste volume by 2045 ambitious.
The source says
The draft sets a 20% municipal waste reduction target per person; 40% applies to waste intensity relative to real GDP.
What it changes
The paragraph overstates the quantity reduction being challenged, making that particular burden argument stronger. It does not demonstrate that the actual 20% target is feasible.

1. What the submission says

„Reduktion des Siedlungsabfallaufkommens um 40 %“

Translation: “reduction of municipal waste volume by 40%”

PDF page 20 (printed 19) · 3.5 Abfallintensität und Abfallvermeidung; paragraph following quoted draft rationale

2. What the source says

BMUKN KrWG June 2026 consultation draft

„Das Pro-Kopf-Aufkommen an Siedlungsabfällen soll bis zum Jahr 2045 im Vergleich zum Jahr 2020 um 20 Prozent sinken.“

Translation: “Municipal waste per capita should fall by 20% by 2045 compared with 2020.”

PDF page 6 · Article 1 number 7, proposed §7b(2) · retrieved 25 October 2026

Open the source ↗

3. The correction

Proposed §7b(2) sets a 20% per-capita municipal-waste reduction. Section 7 b(1) sets 40% lower waste intensity, defined by§3(7 c) as net waste divided by price-adjusted GDP. The quoted 40% rationale on draft page 27 explains intensity, not the separate municipal-waste quantity target.

4. What this does not show

The response correctly reproduces the 20% operative text immediately above and correctly describes 40% intensity elsewhere. A local drafting or reference slip is therefore plausible; readers can recover the correct distinction from the same document. No prediction about feasibility or motive is inferred.

Minor: it doubles the stated quantity-reduction percentage in one supporting paragraph, but the same page gives the correct 20% rule and the response separately recognises the 40% intensity target.
