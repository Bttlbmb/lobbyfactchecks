They want
Finance maintenance from taxes, fees and contributions, with tighter fiscal priorities.
They said
The existing federal annual seaport contribution is EUR 38.4 billion.
The source says
The identifiable statutory grant is EUR 38.346 million, about one-thousandth as much.
What it changes
Greatly reduces the scale of the illustrative existing annual baseline that the author says new infrastructure borrowing could free for social spending. It does not establish whether budget substitution occurs.

3. The correction

Section 1 BFinHBRuaG sets this specific federal grant for seaport infrastructure investment at EUR 38.346 million annually. The submitted billion-euro unit is approximately 1,000 times larger. This does not count every possible federal expense related to ports, waterways or maritime transport.

4. What this does not show

A million/billion clerical slip is a strong explanation. No explicit derived total depends on it, and the wider financing recommendation stands independently. Neither actual diversion of funds nor the entirety of federal maritime expenditure is established. No intent inferred.

Minor despite approximately 1,000-fold unit mismatch: this is an auxiliary illustration, without a derived financing total or quantitative policy threshold. The general maintenance-financing argument does not turn on its amount.
