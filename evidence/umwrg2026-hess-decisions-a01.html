Environmental Appeals Act (UmwRG)

Expert statement by Franziska Heß

Revising environmental legal remedies, including examples of abusive late objections.

found 25 October 2026

In short

They want
Delete the proposed non-participation example of abuse and retain the current open clause.
They said
Both identified Stuttgart decisions denied abusiveness.
The source says
One decision left that question undecided and dismissed the application on its merits. The other did reject abuse.
What it changes
Weakens the small empirical claim that both tested cases affirmatively cleared the associations of abusiveness. It does not alter the reported count of cases in which the abuse clause actually excluded an objection.

Correction in detail

The 12 January 2023 decision left the legal assessment of the association’s prior non-participation undecided; it rejected interim relief on the substantive merits. The 12 October 2023 decision rejected abuse because prior knowledge had not been established. The cited UBA report itself distinguishes an undecided assessment from a rejection, although its first/second ordering does not match the chronological order of its footnote.

What this does not show

The unresolved judgment does not establish that abuse occurred. Neither this correction nor the two cases proves widespread abuse, the desirability or legality of the proposed expansion, or the absence of other reasons to retain the existing clause. No intent is inferred.

Why minor: an affirmative rejection has been substituted for one undecided question, but no objection was excluded for abuse in either case. The broader no-application result and independent legal objections remain.
