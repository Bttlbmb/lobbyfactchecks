3. The correction

The NKR reports €26.8 billion for its cumulative change in recurring compliance costs since 2011, including a €9.3 billion increase in 2022/23. The submission itself correctly gives €26.8 billion on page 2. Holding its €682 million numerator and chosen NKR comparator fixed yields 682/26,800 × 100 = 2.5448%, rather than 2.8655%.

4. What this does not show

This repairs the stated numerical baseline and resulting fraction only. The NKR series is a cumulative change since 2011, not all existing bureaucracy costs. The draft’s €682 million is business relief, while the comparator includes all three addressee groups. No complete comparable economy-wide relief rate or actual realised saving is established. A copying or editing slip is plausible; no intent inferred.

Minor: a roughly 0.32 percentage-point difference leaves the overall small-share argument intact, and the correct baseline appears earlier in the same document.
