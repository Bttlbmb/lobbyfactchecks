Correction in detail

For physician-provided homeopathy, Swiss basic-insurance coverage was suspended from 1 July 2005, restored provisionally in 2012 and made permanent in 2017. It was not continuously permanent from 2005.

What this does not show

Swiss coverage exists today. The chronology correction does not establish clinical ineffectiveness or decide German reimbursement policy. Medicinal products have separate reimbursement rules; the claim is assessed as a statement about the medical method, consistent with the submission’s treatment focus.

Why minor: the historical precedent is overstated, while current Swiss coverage and the author’s other arguments remain.
