Minor error Works against the author’s request

Immediate investment tax programme

Expert statement by Dirk Meyer

Tax changes intended to encourage business investment, including preferential treatment of electric business cars.

submitted 20 June 2025 · found 25 October 2026

In short

They want
Oppose the specific preferential treatment of electric business cars.
They said
The qualifying vehicle-price ceiling rises from €75,000 to €100,000.
The source says
The exact addressed draft raises it from €70,000, so the expansion is €30,000 rather than €25,000.
What it changes
The correction makes the price-ceiling expansion larger; it does not undermine the author’s criticism of preferential treatment.

Correction in detail

The existing qualifying gross-list-price ceiling is €70,000. The explicitly addressed bill raises it to €100,000. The nominal expansion is therefore €30,000, not the €25,000 implied by the submission’s baseline. The separate 75% first-year depreciation rule is a different provision.

What this does not show

No conclusion about take-up, tax revenue, consumer behaviour or the desirability of the measure. A clerical number slip is plausible; no intent is inferred. The criticism of luxury consumption and other policy premises remain.

Why minor: the existing threshold is misstated by €5,000, but the proposed €100,000 endpoint is correct and the overall criticism remains. This is separate from its direction.
