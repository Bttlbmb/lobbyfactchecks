Annual Tax Act 2026

Statement by DSTG

Annual federal tax changes, including administration and EU VAT rules.

The source’s wording

European Commission: VAT in the Digital Age

“Digital Reporting Requirements will affect cross-border B2B transactions from 1 July 2030.”

VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA), opening timeline paragraph · retrieved 24 October 2026

Open the source ↗

Correction in detail

The adopted 2025 package sets 1 July 2030 for general cross-border B 2 B digital reporting. Genuine 2028 platform and single-registration measures have different scope. The original 2022 proposal used 2028, which may explain the outdated date without establishing intent.

What this does not show

Early domestic reporting is permitted, and preparing infrastructure now may still be justified by fraud prevention and implementation lead time. The correction does not disprove the policy request or its longer implementation path; it only weakens the stated EU timetable premise.

Major editorial judgment: the erroneous EU deadline advances the relevant mandatory cross-border implementation horizon by at least two years and materially compresses the preparation period presented to the reader. Independent reasons for early national preparation remain.
