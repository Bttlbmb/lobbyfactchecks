Correction in detail

Replace the universal immediate 80% description with the applicable commissioning-date and operating-age cohorts. The exact draft § 3(3) preserves the relevant start date and years of biomass use; its explanation physical 34 says older plants roll into the 80% requirement after 15 years. Pre-submission Commission guidance table 1 expressly gives 70% until 31 December 2029 for biomass-fuel installations ≥10 MW starting between 1 January 2021 and 20 November 2023, followed by 80% from 2030. Newer plants and certain older cohorts can indeed face 80% in 2026.

What this does not show

This does not establish that affected old plants face no immediate burden, that compliance supplies are readily available, or that a transition is unnecessary. Optional grandfathering under Article 29(15) and automatic cohort thresholds under Article 29(10) are distinct; the finding does not resolve the acknowledged legal debate over applying the former in EU ETS 1. No conclusion about actual E.ON fleet composition or total affected capacity is drawn.

Minor: the universal scope is overstated, but genuinely affected older/newer cohorts and independent implementation concerns remain. This is not an estimate of the number or proportion spared.
