Annual Tax Act 2026

Statement by AmCham Germany Tax Committee

The annual package of German tax changes.

American chamber of commerce · found 25 October 2026

In short

They want
Convert employee-tax thresholds into allowances and simplify staff-event taxation.
They said
The €110 staff-event rule is a threshold whose small excess can cause disproportionate tax.
The source says
The cited income-tax provision already exempts the first €110 for qualifying events.
What it changes
Weakens one stated cliff-edge rationale for changing existing income-tax rules.

Their wording

„Freigrenzen (z. B. die 110-Euro-Grenze bei Betriebsveranstaltungen“

English: “exemption thresholds (e.g. the €110 limit for staff events”

„Soweit solche Zuwendungen den Betrag von 110 Euro je Betriebsveranstaltung und teilnehmenden Arbeitnehmer nicht übersteigen“

Correction in detail

For qualifying events, only the excess above €110 is employment income. The €50 benefits-in-kind example is a genuine threshold; this finding concerns only the €110 example.

What this does not show

Does not establish that event administration is simple or that the separate 10% flat-tax proposal is unwise. VAT has a different €110 threshold; the passage expressly cites income-tax §19. Eligibility conditions and the two-event limit still apply.

Minor: one example in a broader reform programme is wrong. It implies a tax cliff where the cited provision already supplies the requested allowance.
