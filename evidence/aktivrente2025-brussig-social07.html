Aktivrente for working pensioners

Expert statement by Martin Brussig

A tax exemption for qualifying employment income after statutory retirement age.

hearing 1 December 2025 · found 25 October 2026

In short

They want
Reject the Aktivrente and improve working conditions and support for healthy ageing instead.
They said
The German microcensus surveys 0.1 percent of the resident population.
The source says
Its basic annual sample is about 1 percent. This is a narrow survey-description error, not a demonstrated error in the author’s estimates.
What it changes
Corrects a background description of the data used in the argument. A larger basic survey sample would ordinarily support rather than undermine potential statistical precision, but the policy estimates cannot be recalculated from this sentence.

Their wording

„Im Mikrozensus werden 0,1 Prozent der Wohnbevölkerung in Deutschland befragt.“

English: “The microcensus surveys 0.1 percent of the resident population in Germany.”

Correction in detail

Describe the basic annual microcensus selection as approximately 1 percent, not 0.1 percent. Separate this from the realised response, individual subprogrammes, scientific-use-file subsamples and the author’s own filtered analysis sample, none of whose exact sizes is established by the correction.

What this does not show

No finding that the author’s weighted pension or employment totals are wrong, or that every variable or analytic subgroup uses the full 1 percent. Sampling fractions alone do not establish representativeness, causal validity or policy effectiveness. No intent is inferred.

Minor: a factor-ten error in the background sampling fraction, but no demonstrated effect on a published estimate, standard error or policy conclusion. The size of the numerical factor is separate from materiality.
