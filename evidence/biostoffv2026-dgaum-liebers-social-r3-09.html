Correction in detail

The publisher-deposited record for JTEHB 23(7), pages 293–318 identifies a 2020 review by Cochard, Ledoux and Landkocz about atmospheric particles and epithelial transition, DOI 10.1080/10937404.2020.1816238. The printed 293–312 span falls inside this different article. Springer’s original 2020 Liebers review concerns occupational endotoxin effects but is Archives of Toxicology 94:3629–3644, DOI 10.1007/s00204-020-02905-0. This is a possible bibliographic conflation, not a proved identification of the intended source.

What this does not show

No assertion that endotoxin or mixed exposures are harmless, that no Liebers review exists, or that the proposed occupational-medical clarification is wrong. No causal synergy estimate has been independently refuted. Metadata for the article occupying the stated journal pages was read; its full26-page text was not obtained (publisher access 403). The real Liebers publisher abstract and bibliographic header were read; no full review of its clinical evidence is claimed.

Minor bibliographic verification error. Genuine relevant endotoxin literature exists, so the direction of any correction to the policy argument is unknown; no major material effect established.
