Major error Favours the author’s request

krwg 2026

Statement by ITAD response 7 August 2026 to KrWG draft 26 June 2026

Revising waste prevention, recycling and collection rules.

found 25 October 2026

They want
Reassess the draft measures and data basis.
They said
The 69% statistical recycling rate is the WFD compliance basis, suggesting the target is already met.
The source says
Eurostat separates that statistical series from mandatory target reporting, which gives 46.36% for Germany 2023.
What it changes
Removes this numerical rebuttal to the draft’s rationale that further action is needed for the rising EU targets. The series distinction supports the existence of a target gap on the relevant 2023 baseline, without proving that any particular proposed measure is required.

3. The correction

The approximately 69% number is supported by the longstanding statistical JQ series; it is not the rate in the mandatory MWRO series used for the 2025/2030/2035 targets. Eurostat Figure 6 gives Germany 2023 MWRO 46.3603% versus JQ 68.6760%. The exact draft below 50% rationale is consistent with MWRO. This does not decide compliance with the separate old 2020 target, for which earlier calculation options remained relevant.

4. What this does not show

Does not establish 2025 actual compliance, future 2035 performance, the optimal target level, reliable cross-country rankings, or the merits of the requested data improvements. Destatis itself expressly publishes the already-met 2035 claim in its current official explanation; faithful repetition is a strong source-provenance explanation. No intent or invented number is inferred.

Major: the wrong compliance measure turns a baseline below 50% into one above the 2035 threshold 65%, directly reversing a substantial stated reason for the draft measures. This materiality is separate from the direction and from other reasons for data-quality reform.
