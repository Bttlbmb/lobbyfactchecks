Minor error Favours the author’s request

Grid connection package

Statement by BEE

Grid connection priorities, congestion rules and digital connection procedures.

renewable energy federation · submitted 30 September 2026 · found 25 October 2026

They want
Require current network data and faster, uniform digital grid procedures.
They said
Grey areas in a regulator map are among regions whose grid operators lag dramatically in digitalisation.
The source says
The map defines grey as missing data or an index that could not be calculated, rather than measured poor digitalisation.
What it changes
Weakens the claim that the reproduced map demonstrates dramatic digitalisation deficits in the grey areas. The other coloured areas may still support the author’s broader concern.

3. The correction

The regulator defines grey as unavailable data or an indicator that could not be calculated. It cannot be read as an observed low digitalisation score. The manual also makes clear that the green-to-red scale is relative to values within the dataset; this supports caution about absolute judgments but is not a separate issue here.

4. What this does not show

This does not establish that operators in grey areas are well digitalised, that missing data are unimportant, or that the requested digitalisation and disclosure duties are unwise. It concerns an affirmative description of the map, not inference that absent evidence proves good performance. The paragraph may be intended as a collective regional summary rather than a categorical judgment about each grey operator; independent review considered that alternative but retained the narrow map-description mismatch.

Minor: the error concerns one piece of supporting map evidence for stronger digitalisation duties. It does not invalidate the broader policy case or measured deficits elsewhere.
