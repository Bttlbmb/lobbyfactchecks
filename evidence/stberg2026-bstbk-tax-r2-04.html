Minor error Favours the author’s request

Tax Advisers Act, 9th amendment

Statement by Bundessteuerberaterkammer

Reform of tax-advice permissions and several tax rules, including real-estate transfer tax.

tax advisers’ chamber · submitted 8 April 2026 · found 25 October 2026

In short

They want
Clarify the transfer-tax classification of photovoltaic systems on mounting structures and consider an exemption for certain sizes.
They said
A named 2010 BFH judgment had already classified such photovoltaic systems as business equipment.
The source says
The named judgment concerns pig-barn feeding and ventilation equipment and VAT adjustment periods; it makes no photovoltaic ruling.
What it changes
Removes the claimed direct BFH precedent from the argument for clarifying photovoltaic transfer-tax treatment.

Their wording

„PV-Anlagen auf Trägerkonstruktionen regelmäßig als Betriebsvorrichtungen zu qualifizieren sind“

English: “photovoltaic systems on mounting structures are generally to be classified as business equipment”

PDF page 33 (printed 31) · IV.3(b), paragraph beginning “Umstritten ist hingegen”; the sentence cites BFH XI R 9/09, 14 July 2010.

The source’s wording

BFH XI R 9/09, judgment 14 July 2010, official full text

„Vorsteuerberichtigungszeitraum für eine Fütterungsanlage und eine Lüftungsanlage eines Schweinestalls“

English: “input-VAT adjustment period for a feeding system and a ventilation system in a pig barn”

PDF page 1 · Judgment title; facts paragraphs 1–8 and reasons paragraphs 14, 36–41 substantiate the subject and holding. · retrieved 25 October 2026

Correction in detail

BFH XI R 9/09 decides the adjustment period for input VAT on permanently installed pig-barn feeding and ventilation equipment. It treats that equipment as essential building components and business equipment, and applies a ten-year adjustment period. It contains no ruling about photovoltaic systems on mounting structures. The submission therefore misdescribes the specific authority it names.

What this does not show

This does not establish that the author’s proposed photovoltaic classification is legally wrong, that another judgment could not support it, or that clarification or an exemption would be unwise. Generic reasoning about equipment and building components may support an analogy. The finding concerns the description of this named case, not a comprehensive legal opinion or author intent.

Minor: the cited court authority is materially misdescribed within a specific legal argument, but the correction does not dispose of the requested clarification, the underlying tax classification, or the submission’s wider policy position.
