Early-start pension

Statement by vzbv

A state-funded private-pension starting balance for children, with a collective default investment option.

consumer federation · submitted 22 September 2026 · found 25 October 2026

They want
Bind the collective investment strategy more tightly in the statute, including broad diversification, passive management and low costs.
They said
The government bill already requires the collective fund to invest exclusively in globally diversified stocks or stock funds.
The source says
The addressed September government bill does not impose the stock-only requirement. That appeared in the earlier July consultation draft and was later restored in October.
What it changes
The original government bill leaves more statutory investment discretion than described. Correcting that description strengthens, rather than defeats, the request to anchor additional investment safeguards directly in law.

3. The correction

The September government bill requires global diversification and the principles of return, security and liquidity; its §24(4) delegates eligible asset classes to guidelines. It does not require exclusive stock investment. The exact July 21 consultation version did impose that restriction, while the October 14 committee recommendation later restored an exclusive broad-global-stock rule. Neither is the government bill addressed at the October 5 hearing.

4. What this does not show

This is a narrow version-description slip. It does not show that the collective fund would actually invest in a particular non-equity asset, that a stock-only allocation is preferable, or that passive management and low costs follow automatically from that restriction. The rest of the requested safeguards remains a policy judgment. No intent is inferred.

Minor: this overstates one existing constraint in a short investment-policy subsection; it does not alter the proposed public/default arrangement or undermine the separate requests for passive management, broad diversification and low costs.
