Minor error Favours the author’s request

Private pension reform

Statement by vzbv

Reform of tax-subsidised private pension saving, including new pension depots and a limit on qualifying contracts.

consumer federation · submitted 12 March 2026 · found 25 October 2026

In short

They want
Clarify that the two-contract limit applies only to newly concluded contracts, protecting existing pension arrangements.
They said
Concluding a third contract makes the first contract lose pension-contract status.
The source says
The addressed bill excludes the third and subsequent contracts. The author’s own proposed wording on the same page already says third.
What it changes
Removes the stated first-contract-disqualification mechanism from the argument that older advantageous contracts might be pushed out through new sales. It narrows one rationale for the requested wording clarification.

Correction in detail

The exact government bill links the loss of qualifying contribution and contract treatment to the third and subsequent contracts, ordered by conclusion date. It does not disqualify the first contract when a third is concluded. Its explanatory memorandum also expressly excludes contracts concluded before 1 January 2027.

What this does not show

The same page’s proposed wording correctly refers to the third contract, making an editorial slip a strong explanation. The request may still be defensible as added clarity. This does not establish that all old-contract sales or replacement risks disappear, that the two-contract cap is desirable, or that the author’s wider consumer-policy analysis is false. No intent is inferred.

Minor: this reverses which contract is affected in one supporting explanation, but the author’s own request already uses the correct third-contract rule. It does not overturn the requested clarification or the broader reform position.
