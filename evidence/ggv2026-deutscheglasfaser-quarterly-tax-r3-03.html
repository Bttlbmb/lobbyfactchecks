gigabit grundbuch verordnung 2026

Statement by Deutsche Glasfaser

Data reporting and access rules for the German Gigabit-Grundbuch infrastructure portal.

company · found 25 October 2026

They want
Permit changed-data reports instead of repeated full datasets and reduce reporting burdens.
They said
The draft requires a full delivery every three months.
The source says
Its three-month mechanism is a possible no-change confirmation, distinct from required full updates.
What it changes
Removes the claimed quarterly full-delivery requirement where nothing changed, weakening that part of the burden argument.

3. The correction

Section 12(1) requires the updated full dataset when the EU update duty applies. Section 12(2) allows the authority to request no-change confirmation no sooner than three months after the last delivery. The explanatory note expressly calls this confirmation a relief.

4. What this does not show

Full updated datasets remain required when data changes, potentially more often than quarterly. Checking that nothing changed can itself require work. No actual implementation burden or optimum reporting method is established, and differential reporting may remain a defensible request.

Minor: the response repeatedly overstates one mechanism, but the separate criticism of full updates under (1) survives.
