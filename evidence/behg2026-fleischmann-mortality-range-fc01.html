Fuel emissions trading amendment (BEHG)

Expert statement by Bernd Fleischmann

Extending national fuel-carbon pricing through the transition to the European system.

found 25 October 2026

They want
Abolish the national fuel-emissions trading law.
They said
Every European country has five to fifteen times more cold-related than heat-related deaths.
The source says
The cited study’s corrected urban estimates show widely differing ratios, including values above 30 and below five. They do not establish a universal national range.
What it changes
Corrects the universal numerical range in a supporting health comparison. The deviations are mixed, the study still estimates a larger aggregate cold burden, and the approximate German six-to-one ratio is supported within its urban cohort. No uniform direction of policy advantage is established.

3. The correction

Describe the corrected study’s estimates for adults aged 20 and older in 854 urban areas during 2000–2019. Its country-grouped point estimates imply cold/heat ratios of about 31.6 for Denmark (884/28), 32.3 for Finland (1452/45), 58.1 for the UK (28342/488), and 3.9 for Croatia (1142/293). They do not support an everywhere-five-to-fifteen description. The corrected German ratio 12108/2015 is about 6.0.

4. What this does not show

This is a narrow overgeneralisation of cited evidence, not proof of a different whole-population ratio in every country. The estimates are modelled temperature-attributable mortality with uncertainty, not counts of direct hypothermia and heatstroke deaths. The study was corrected on 2 July 2024; corrected values are used. A historical cold/heat comparison does not by itself determine the effect of future warming, adaptation or emissions policy.

Minor: corrects a universal numerical range and the population scope of a supporting health comparison. It preserves the cited study’s larger aggregate cold burden and does not independently decide the policy request.
