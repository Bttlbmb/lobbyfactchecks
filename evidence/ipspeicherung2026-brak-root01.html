3. The correction

The court describes data already stored under the 2007 statute and the 2008 interim restrictions on their use. The regime operated before the 2 March 2010 judgment invalidated it. January 2026 is less than 16 years after that date, so a continuous absence of actual retention for over 18 years is incorrect. This does not assert uninterrupted implementation by every provider before 2010 or disregard interim use restrictions.

4. What this does not show

Minor chronology issue only. The statement says de facto, so this is checked against actual stored data rather than merely a statute’s existence. One possible loose reading is that no fully constitutionally valid retention scheme existed; that differs from the express claim that no retention was conducted. The correction does not establish necessity, proportionality, legality, effectiveness or appropriate duration of the 2025 draft. Its separate 90.8% clearance quotation and broader rights objections are not refuted.

Minor: the overstatement is a little over two years in a subsidiary historical premise, and the much longer absence since 2010 still supports discussing alternatives. No substantive legal conclusion turns on 18 rather than fewer than 16 years.
