3. The correction

At the 19 percentVATrate,€1.77 gross is€1.48739496 net. Comparing like with like gives€8.35−€1.77/1.19=€6.86260504 net, approximately€6.86, rather than€6.58. Equivalently the gross remainder is€8.35×1.19−€1.77=€8.1665.

4. What this does not show

The fixed remainder is not total pharmacy remuneration: the percentage component and other payments exist. This arithmetic does not determine an adequate fee, validate a 40 percentuplift, establish profitability, or resolve legal arguments. Correcting the units does not itself oppose the requested fee increase. No intent is inferred.

Minor: roughly 28 cents or 4.1 percentof the correct current net fixed remainder. The arithmetic feeds a fee illustration but does not resolve the broader economic request.
