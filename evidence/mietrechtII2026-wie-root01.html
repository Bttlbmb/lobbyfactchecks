Minor error Favours the author’s request

Tenancy law reform II

Statement by Wohnen im Eigentum

Regulating short-term and furnished rentals, index-linked rents and protection against termination after arrears are paid.

homeowners’ association · submitted 6 March 2026 · found 25 October 2026

They want
Replace the proposed six-month limit with a rebuttable presumption or a longer limit.
They said
The draft makes rental duration the only criterion for the short-term exception and removes consideration of the particular purpose.
The source says
The operative draft expressly retains a special-need criterion alongside the six-month duration limit.
What it changes
Removes the stated premise that the draft abandons examination of the particular rental purpose. This weakens that part of the justification for replacing the proposed definition.

1. What the submission says

„Es käme danach nur noch auf die Dauer und nicht den konkreten Anlass an.“

Translation: “It would then depend only on the duration and no longer on the particular occasion.”

2. What the source says

„Wohnraum, der vom Mieter für einen besonderen Bedarf für einen Zeitraum von bis zu sechs Monaten angemietet wird“

Translation: “residential premises rented by the tenant for a special need for a period of up to six months”

3. The correction

The draft requires both a special need and a rental duration of no more than six months. Its explanatory memorandum explains the special-purpose criterion on pages 20–21. It adds a maximum duration; it does not replace the purpose criterion with duration alone.

4. What this does not show

The six-month maximum still excludes rentals exceeding that duration even where a special purpose exists. The author can therefore maintain its separate objection to a rigid maximum and its request for a presumption or longer period. This finding does not establish the appropriate duration or decide whether any individual rental should qualify. No intent is inferred.

Minor editorial judgment: an express description of the operative test is wrong, but correcting it leaves the adjacent rigid-duration objection and central policy request largely intact. This is narrower than a finding that the whole short-term-rental argument fails.
