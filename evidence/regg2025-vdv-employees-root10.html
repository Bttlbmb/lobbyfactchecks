Correction in detail

The BA September 2025 report, published on 30 September before the 1 October submission, estimated 34,768,800 employees subject to social-insurance contributions in July 2025. This is about 13.2 million below the claimed 48 million. Its broader all-employed category was 45.95 million in August, which is a different population; the case does not identify where the author obtained 48 million.

What this does not show

Minor category/size correction only. The job ticket may also be available to workers outside the stated social-insurance category; 34.77 million is not a demonstrated cap on eligibility or demand. The BA estimate was provisional and lagged the submission by two months. A typographical error or loose shorthand for a broader working population is plausible. No financial-impact calculation or policy rejection follows, and no intent is inferred.

Why minor: the count substantially overstates the specifically named category, but is an auxiliary scale argument, not a calculated uptake, budget, or benefit estimate. The large remaining group and independent planning-certainty reasons still support discussion of the request.
