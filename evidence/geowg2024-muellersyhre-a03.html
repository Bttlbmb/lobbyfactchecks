Geothermal and heat-pump acceleration act

Expert statement by Reinhard Müller-Syhre

Accelerating geothermal heat, heat-pump and heat-storage permits.

submitted 31 October 2024 · found 25 October 2026

They want
Reject the whole bill.
They said
A 33 ct household electricity input and 0.347 heat/electricity ratio produce a monetary ratio of 1.592.
The source says
The official electricity average is 42.29 ct; also 9.5/33 is 0.28788. Correcting both inputs yields about 1.031 under the same model.
What it changes
Lowers the stated monetary heat-output/electricity-cost ratio. Both errors made the hypothetical heat-pump case appear more favourable.

3. The correction

Two defects affect one monetary comparator: the first-half 2023 household electricity average is 42.29 ct/kWh, not 33.00; independently, the stated 9.5/33 division is 0.28788, not 0.347. Correcting the division alone gives 1.32136; correcting both gives approximately 1.031 under the same remaining assumptions.

4. What this does not show

Actual operator tariffs, delivery costs and total project profitability remain unknown. The historical household average is not automatically an industrial tariff. The separate below-one conclusion stays unresolved. Either a misplaced input or arithmetic typo could explain the defects; no intent is inferred.

Minor editorial judgment: two clear defects in one supporting monetary comparator. Their combined correction is substantial within that model, but the household-price comparison does not determine commercial project returns.
