Air travel tax amendment

Statement by BDI

Reducing German air passenger tax from July 2026.

industry federation · submitted 17 March 2026 · found 24 October 2026

In short

They want
Restore the ETS-linked annual tax abatement mechanism.
They said
The cited 2019 tax amendment entered force on 1 April 2024.
The source says
It entered force on 1 April 2020; this is a chronology error.
What it changes
Corrects the history. It does not materially weaken the request for reinstatement.

Their wording

„bereits zum Zeitpunkt des Inkrafttretens am 1. April 2024“

English: “already at the time of entry into force on 1 April 2024”

Correction in detail

Replace 2024 with 2020. The official government report confirms both commencement and contemporaneous abatement.

What this does not show

The adjacent argument that statutory rates were reduced through the previous abatement mechanism remains supported. A clerical year error is a plausible explanation. No intent inferred.

Why minor: a wrong historical year leaves the described mechanism and comparison of statutory versus effective rates intact.
