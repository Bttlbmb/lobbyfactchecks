Minor error Favours the author’s request

Income Tax Reform Act 2027

Statement by Deutscher Steuerberaterverband (DStV)

Income-tax changes including raising the employee expense allowance from EUR 1,230 to EUR 1,430.

tax advisers’ association · found 25 October 2026

In short

They want
Give priority to a workday allowance instead of merely raising the existing employee allowance.
They said
Every compulsory-assessment case requires the employment-income attachment regardless of expenses.
The source says
Official instructions permit omission of that attachment when electronic wage data suffice and specified extra entries are unnecessary. A compulsory return alone does not remove that exception.
What it changes
This weakens the categorical procedural objection to the allowance increase as a simplification. It does not validate the government estimate that 1.3 million taxpayers will omit the attachment, nor settle the comparison with a workday allowance.

„ist die Anlage N unabhängig von der Höhe der tatsächlich angefallenen Werbungskosten einzureichen.“

Translation of claim fragment: “Anlage N must be submitted regardless of the amount of actual employment expenses.”

Correction in detail

The official 2025 instructions for unrestricted taxpayers dispense with Anlage N where wage data were transmitted electronically, no entries are needed in lines 10, 18–19 or 21–26, and employment expenses do not exceed the applicable allowance. Those conditions can coexist with compulsory assessment, for example ordinary electronic wage data for spouses using III/V. The requirement to submit a tax return is distinct from the requirement to complete this attachment.

What this does not show

Some compulsory-assessment cases do require extra entries and an attachment; the finding concerns the universal assertion, not every example. Higher expenses after a move or job change can still require entries. Existing 2025 guidance is not a complete specification of future 2027 forms. No claim about actual filing behaviour, total time saved, distributional fairness or the preferred allowance is established.

Why minor: one concrete procedural premise overstates the remaining filing burden. The overall reform request also rests on separate simplicity and fairness arguments, and the numerical government forecast is not independently tested.
