Correction in detail

Distinguish 2013 personal-possession and regulatory implementation from the opening of licensed recreational retail stores on 1 January 2014. Medical sales predated both.

The legal framework and licence applications developed in 2013, which may explain the date conflation. The chronology correction does not show a causal effect of retail sales on crashes. The separate causal-description issue remains distinct.
