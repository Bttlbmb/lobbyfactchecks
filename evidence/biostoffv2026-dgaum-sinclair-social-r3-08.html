Correction in detail

In Journal of Safety Research volume 46, pages 145–155 identify Boelhouwer, Davis, Franco-Watkins, Dorris and Lungu’s 2013 paper on hazard pictograms, DOI 10.1016/j.jsr.2013.06.001. Elsevier-deposited metadata and the coauthor’s university record agree. The author’s stated 145–150 interval lies within that different paper, and the printed Sinclair author/title combination is bibliographically inconsistent. The intended Sinclair reference has not been established.

What this does not show

No claim that no Sinclair training research exists or that the recommendation for practical instruction is wrong. A copied, blended or partly mistyped citation is plausible. Original article metadata and university-hosted abstract were read; the complete11-page Boelhouwer paper was not obtained or clinically reappraised. Preliminary secondary catalogue inference that these pages were a railway paper was rejected when primary metadata showed otherwise; it is not used as evidence.

Minor source-location error affecting verification of a supporting reference. It does not demonstrate that the practical-training request changes or that claimed learning effects are false.
