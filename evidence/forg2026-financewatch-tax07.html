Correction in detail

Replace March 2024 with March 2023 for the emergency crisis and takeover announcement. FINMA records the announcement on 19 March 2023 and legal completion on 12 June 2023.

What this does not show

No conclusion about the sufficiency of the bill’s safeguards. This does not refute the claimed Archegos connection or imply intent; a clerical year slip is plausible. Collapse is treated as ordinary crisis shorthand, not a claim of formal bankruptcy.

Why minor: the year does not change the existence of the example, its relationship to the earlier Archegos episode, or the policy mechanism under discussion.
