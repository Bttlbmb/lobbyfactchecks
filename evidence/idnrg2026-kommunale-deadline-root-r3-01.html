In short

They want
Shorten the proposed 2032 implementation deadline for land-register data.
They said
Other registers have a 31 August 2028 implementation deadline.
The source says
The statutory general deadline is 31 December 2028, as the exact draft explains. The gap is four months smaller, but remains four years.
What it changes
Slightly weakens the timetable argument for earlier implementation: the gap between the general deadline and the proposed land-register deadline is four years, rather than four years and four months.

Correction in detail

IDNrG §2 no.1 requires storage by the end of the fifth calendar year following commencement. Commencement on 31 August 2023 means a deadline of 31 December 2028. The exact draft expressly gives that date. The proposed deadline for land-register data remains the end of 2032.

What this does not show

The four-year gap remains substantial. Does not establish that 2032 is appropriate or that the separate requests for cadastral inclusion, technical access and funding are mistaken. No intent inferred.

Minor timetable-comparison error. Correcting four months leaves a four-year gap and preserves the central argument for synchronisation. No substantial reversal is established.
