Digital violence act

Statement by Stiftung Digitale Chancen

Civil and criminal protection against digital violence.

foundation · submitted 22 May 2026 · found 24 October 2026

They want
Involve young people and adapt the law to minors’ needs.
They said
The cited study measured 231 minutes online daily.
The source says
The measure is smartphone screen time for a defined respondent subset.
What it changes
Corrects what the cited usage figure measures. It cannot quantify the total time minors were online or their exposure to digital violence.

1. What the submission says

„waren die befragten jungen Menschen 231 Minuten täglich online.“

Translation: “the young people surveyed were online for 231 minutes daily.”

3. The correction

JIM 2025 reports smartphone screen time, read from the device by respondents able to do so (n=960), rather than total time online across devices. Its methods section explicitly says it stopped reporting unspecific online time. The numeral 231 matches; the measured variable and sample need to be described accurately.

4. What this does not show

Does not establish that actual total online time was above or below 231 minutes: smartphones can be used offline and the internet can be used on other devices. It does not rebut the submission’s separately sourced harassment figures or the case for child-sensitive procedures.

Minor: the number is transcribed correctly, while its label and population are broadened. The general case for responding to minors’ needs survives.
