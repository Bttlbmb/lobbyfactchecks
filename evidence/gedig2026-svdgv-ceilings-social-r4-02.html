3. The correction

The first-day claim is too broad if it means a binding ceiling constrains every DiGA from its first reimbursed sale. Under the framework’s ordinary group rules, § 3d expressly exempts up to 2,000 redeemed codes per DiGA ID in the first year. This is not a universal exemption for every route: the fallback-group treatment differs. The GKV implementation page, last updated 5 October 2026 before this submission, repeats the initial exemption. The framework also contains defined category exceptions. A legal ceiling framework exists, but that does not establish an effective ceiling for every application from day one.

4. What this does not show

The strongest benign reading is shorthand for the applicability of the general framework and group assignment from listing, rather than a claim that every sale is immediately capped. The association correctly identifies a legally mandated, jointly negotiated framework. Nevertheless its universal first-day wording is used to rebut first-year pricing discretion without acknowledging the express initial exemption. This narrow issue does not establish that the GKV report is unbiased, that every manufacturer can charge any amount indefinitely, that a particular price was lawful or unlawful, or that the reporting proposal is unwise. No intent inferred.

Minor: the scope error concerns one specific rebuttal in a broader request about reporting governance. No aggregate price effect or patient impact is established. Materiality is independent of its direction.
