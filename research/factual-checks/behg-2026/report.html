9 previously examined external responses, 60 physical PDF pages (including committee covers where present). Material factual claims are checked irrespective of whether an author cites a source. The ledger records 194 claim assessments. Every initial inventory received independent peer review, followed by final adjudication.

70 unresolved · 123 supported · 1 overstated

“Supported” concerns the specific bounded proposition. “Unresolved” is an assessed uncertainty, not an error or confirmation.

Results

  • All nine archived external responses and all 60 physical PDF pages were re-read; 190 initial claims received complete independent peer review. Root separately adjudicated five peer omissions, extending one existing assessment and adding four premises.
  • One narrow minor overstatement is admitted: the corrected cited urban mortality table does not support a five-to-fifteen cold/heat ratio in every country group. Its larger aggregate cold burden and the German cohort ratio remain intact. The direction of the correction is unknown because country deviations are mixed.
  • No new major finding is admitted. The infrasound threshold, net-feedback and measured-versus-modelled candidates remain unresolved after fair contextual alternatives and source-scope limits were tested.
  • The earlier public July consultation draft, official auction series, coalition agreement and technology-specific EEG payment table resolved several access gaps in favour of supported assessments. Unresolved assessments remain separate from main issue counts.
