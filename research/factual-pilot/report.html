Factual review pilot · 25 October 2026 · Independent AI checks

Expanded factual checks of the CO₂ pipeline consultation

Seven previously examined external responses, 35 author pages and 111 recorded claims. Every claim inventory was checked by another researcher, with final adjudication recorded separately.

75 supported · 35 unresolved · 1 overstated

“Supported” concerns the specific bounded proposition. “Unresolved” is an assessed uncertainty, not an error or confirmation.

Results

  • The final ledger contains 111 claim assessments: 75 supported, 35 unresolved and one narrow overstatement. All 108 initial assessments were independently peer-reviewed; three additional bounded premises were independently adjudicated by the lead reviewer.
  • One new provisional minor finding: transport pipelines do not cross state borders by definition. There is no new major finding, and the case for nationwide uniform rules remains.
  • Primary engineering evidence corroborates several uncited factual premises about phase behaviour, corrosion/impurities and running ductile fracture. Costs, detailed standard adequacy and several legal interpretations remain unresolved.
  • A proposed error concerning CCS necessity was not admitted after independent review: the 2022 report’s selected-scenario conclusions and the 2045 versus 2050 distinction support a reasonable contextual reading.
  • This is deeper verification of the same seven submissions, not new document coverage or a national error-rate estimate.
